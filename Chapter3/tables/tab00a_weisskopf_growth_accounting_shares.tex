\textit{Notes:} Logarithmic growth-accounting decomposition grounded in \citet[Section 6.1, Eqs.~28--31]{Basu2017} and \citet{Weisskopf1978,Weisskopf1979}. By mathematical definition, the annual rate of change in the profit rate satisfies $\Delta \ln r \equiv \Delta \ln(1 - \omega) + \Delta \ln(P_Y / P_K) + \Delta \ln \mu + \Delta \ln y^p - \Delta \ln k$ identically. Component contributions correspond to: (1) Distribution: growth in the profit share ($\Delta \ln[1 - \omega]$); (2) Relative price: change in the internal terms of trade between output and capital equipment deflators ($\Delta \ln[P_Y / P_K]$); (3) Realization: change in structural capacity utilization ($\Delta \ln \mu$), estimated via Chapter~2 CMPR IM-OLS; (4) Technical efficiency: growth in capacity labor productivity ($\Delta \ln y^p$); and (5) Mechanization: growth in the capital-labor ratio entering with a negative sign ($-\Delta \ln k$). Values in parentheses report each factor's compounding percentage share of the change in the rate of profit ($\text{Share}_i \equiv \Delta \ln X_i / \Delta \ln r \times 100\%$). In Panel~A, rates are annualized compound log differences between regime endpoints. Panel~B reports annual log differences during the Unidad Popular cycle. All rows sum horizontally to $\Delta \ln r$ to machine precision.
