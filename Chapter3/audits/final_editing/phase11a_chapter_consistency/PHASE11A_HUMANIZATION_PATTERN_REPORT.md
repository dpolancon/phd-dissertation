### 2. Forensic Pattern Breakdown

#### 2.1 Contrast Constructions
Across the 86-page monograph, contrastive syntax appears with the following distribution:
- **`rather than` (30 occurrences, ~0.35 per page):**
  - *Analytical Function:* Most instances perform legitimate epistemic demarcation, distinguishing capacity reactivation from Austrian malinvestment ("reactivation rather than intertemporal capital misallocation", §1.6), or structural balance-of-payments constraints from exogenous policy choices ("endogenous accommodation rather than autonomous emission", §3.11).
  - *Evaluation:* **Plausible Authorial / Discipline Habit.** No wholesale elimination is warranted; only 2–3 sentences in Section 2 carry double contrasts that can be smoothed in Phase 11B.
- **`not X, but Y` (8 occurrences):**
  - *Examples:* "was not an inevitable systemic accident, but a contingent political-economic maneuver" (§1.3); "appeared not as structural class conflict, but as a technical distortion" (§2.18).
