# 01 — Prose and Register Ledger

## Editorial stance

This ledger records atomic prose/register operations only. A row does not authorize an empirical change. Claims with empirical consequences are cross-referenced to `02_CLAIM_CALIBRATION_LEDGER.md` or `04_EMPIRICAL_FOLLOWUP_LEDGER.md`.

Severity means editorial priority, not a judgment of the paper's scholarly value.

## Rhetorical / technical repetition map (main text, pp. 1–40; approximate text-extraction counts)
