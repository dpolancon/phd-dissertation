Evaluated via standard Granger causality tests in stationary VAR($k$) specifications. In Panel~A, narrow money Granger-causes base money ($M1 \to H$, $F = 21.453, p < 0.0001$), consistent with an accommodative reserve response to transactions money. The multiplier channel is inactive across tested horizons ($m \not\to \pi$, $p \ge 0.562$), whereas inflation Granger-causes subsequent multiplier compression at multi-month horizons ($\pi \to m$, $F = 5.127, p = 0.0020$ at lag 3).
