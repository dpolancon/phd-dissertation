\textit{Notes:} Author's calculations based on \citet{Astorga2023}, Clio-Lab PUC / \citet{DiazLudersWagner2016}, and \citet{Polanco2026}. Grounded in the Classical-Marxian accumulation framework (\citealt{Weisskopf1978,Weisskopf1979}; \citealt[Section 6.1]{Basu2017}). Following strict perpetual inventory accounting, productive capital is defined as $K_t \equiv K_{\text{ME}, t} + K_{\text{NRC}, t}$ (Machinery \& Equipment plus Non-Residential Construction, strictly excluding Residential Construction). Gross accumulation is $g^{\text{gross}}_{K, t} \equiv I^{\text{gross}}_t / K_{t-1}$; implicit physical depreciation is $D_t \equiv K_{t-1} + I^{\text{gross}}_t - K_t$ with rate $\delta_t \equiv D_t / K_{t-1}$; net accumulation is $g^n_{K, t} \equiv I^{\text{net}}_t / K_{t-1} = g^{\text{gross}}_{K, t} - \delta_t$. Propensity to accumulate out of gross surplus is $\chi_t \equiv P_{K, t} I^{\text{gross}}_t / \Pi^{\text{gross}}_t = g^{\text{gross}}_{K, t} / r_t$, establishing the exact identity $g^n_{K, t} \equiv \chi_t \cdot r_t - \delta_t$. In Panel~A, regime figures are period arithmetic means, with effective regime propensity $\chi^* \equiv \bar{g}^{\text{gross}}_K / \bar{r}$. All rows satisfy the net accumulation identity to machine precision.
